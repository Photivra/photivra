Required notice (exact quote)
-----------------------------
"This product includes DNG technology under license by Adobe."

The licence requires this sentence, displayed prominently, in the source code and
documentation of any distributed implementation. Photivra carries it in NOTICE,
README.md, src/capture/dng-import.ts, src/capture/native-raw-dng.ts and
src/capture/photographic-export.ts.

Summary (our words, not legal advice)
-------------------------------------
- Who receives it: anyone. Adobe grants the licence directly to every person and
  organization, so Photivra does not need to pass it on under Apache-2.0.
- What it covers: only the patent claims Adobe can license free of charge that cannot
  be avoided when software reads or writes files that follow any public version of
  the DNG Specification. It does not cover other technology the specification only
  refers to (for example TIFF, JPEG, Exif, ICC or XMP), general enabling technology,
  or other file formats.
- What it allows: making, using, selling, importing and distributing the parts of a
  product that read or write DNG files, worldwide, with no royalty. The right cannot
  be transferred and is not exclusive.
- Conditions: include the notice above; Adobe may withdraw the licence from anyone
  (or their affiliates) who brings a patent action against Adobe about reading or
  writing DNG files.
- No warranty: Adobe makes no promise that any patent is valid, maintained or
  enforced, or that an implementation infringes no other patent. Adobe keeps all
  rights it does not grant.
